Research

HARF and Lifecycle Guard

My master's thesis investigates hidden reliability failures in Home Assistant automation configurations. HARF organizes these failures into ten families, and Lifecycle Guard is a static analysis prototype designed to detect configuration-level risks and offer repair guidance.

My broader research interests include static analysis, automated repair, configuration debugging, and dependable IoT systems.

Machine Learning Classification of Home Assistant Configuration Issues

I developed a corpus of 2,377 labeled Home Assistant Community threads and compared TF-IDF, DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and zero-shot classification approaches, with a separate 190-case expert-coded evaluation set.
