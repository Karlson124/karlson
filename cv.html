Research experience

Graduate Researcher, Smart-Home Automation Reliability

Auburn University at Montgomery · Advisor: Sheik Murad Hassan Anik, Ph.D. | 2026 – Present

  • Built HARF, a taxonomy of Home Automation Reliability Failures with 10 families, from 664 user reports on the Home Assistant Community forum and GitHub (2018–2026) and 179 further GitHub issues; identified 153 failures activated by lifecycle events such as restarts, reloads, retriggers, and failed actions.
  • Verified 67 platform behaviors against the Home Assistant documentation and source code, recorded in a ledger of facts that grounds each detection rule.
  • Built Lifecycle Guard, a local, deterministic, rule-based analyzer with 57 checks that detects reliability risks in Home Assistant automations, explains and ranks them, and suggests repairs that the user confirms; uses no LLMs or external AI services, to protect household privacy.
  • Designed repairs that preserve or rebuild automation state after interruptions, such as stored deadlines, startup triggers, and restore-capable timers.
  • Evaluated the frozen tool on 113 unseen failures: it found the correct failure family for 46.9% of them, while Home Assistant's own validation flagged none.
  • Tested the repairs on a real Home Assistant installation in a virtual machine; they reached the intended state in 10 of 12 experiments.
  • Measured inter-rater agreement with a second rater on a stratified sample of 211 reports (family κ = 0.74).
